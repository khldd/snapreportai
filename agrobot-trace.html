The Evidence Trace

From new rule
to known impact.

The mark is a visible chain of reasoning, not an abstract promise of intelligence.

Its route borrows the back-and-forth reading pattern of field rows and legal text. Every turn records a handoff: source to requirement, requirement to product, product to a conclusion a person can verify.

01 / ReceiveThe route begins open.

A source notch acknowledges that every answer starts somewhere specific and inspectable.

02 / RegisterEach handoff leaves a point.

Registration dots show where legal text meets business and product context.

03 / ProveThe route closes once.

The terminal ring closes only when the evidence chain is ready to review.
