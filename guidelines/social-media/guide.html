07 / Photography, screenshots, icons

Real work, really happening.

Photography

Use

  • Real places: production lines, labs, warehouses, audits on site, training sessions.
  • People doing the work: hands, tools, labels, checklists, evidence.
  • Natural light, documentary framing, honest colour.
  • Our own team, photographed where they work.

Avoid

  • Stock clichés: handshakes, pointing at laptops, staged smiles.
  • AI-generated people, faces or factories.
  • Robots, glowing brains, binary code, blue holograms.
  • Heavy filters, vignettes, colour washes, gradients.

Icons: Lucide line set, 2 px stroke at 24 px, round ends, one colour (Ink, White or Snap Blue). No emoji or filled clip-art inside graphics.

Product screenshots

● Flat, cropped, framed
↙
✕ Tilted, shadowed, circled
  • Real product, current version. No mock-ups of features that don’t exist.
  • Demo workspace only. No customer names, audit data, emails or personal data, and no test entries like “test” or “asda”.

08  /  Video — Structure & motionSnapReport / Social & content standards

08.1 / Video

Move with intent, then hold.

Most feed videos run 30–60 s (90 s maximum). Longer explainers go to YouTube. Design for sound off: the story must work with captions alone.

0–3 sHook

The problem or the result in 7 words or fewer, on screen and spoken.

3–10 sContext

Who it’s for and why it matters now.

10–40 sProof

The real product doing the job: screen recording or field footage. One task, start to finish.

40–50 sResult

Frame the outcome: the report, the finding, the number.

Last 3 sEnd card

Stacked lockup, one CTA, one URL.

cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
Motion rules
  • Capture once. One scan or framing action per scene to set the focus.
  • Resolve in 900 ms or less, with a fast start and a soft landing (curve, left).
  • Then hold. Text stays still long enough to read twice: about 0.4 s per word, 1.5 s minimum.
  • Signal Current may drift right, slowly (one tile per 24 s). It never reverses.
● Transitions we use
  • Hard cut
  • Frame-in: corners close on the subject
  • Push right, with the Signal Current
  • Scan line in Proof Orange, once per video
✕ Never
  • Spins, 3D flips, page curls
  • Glitch, zoom blur, light leaks
  • Bouncy or elastic easing
  • Logo intros before the hook

10  /  Compliance — Claims, privacy, accessSnapReport / Social & content standards

10.1 / Before anything goes live

Evidence before assertion.

Claims

Every claim needs proof on file.

  • Every number needs a source you can show: a documented internal measurement, a customer-approved figure or a public study. Save it with the post as sources.txt.
  • Written sign-off before publishing: performance figures (time saved, accuracy, %); security, hosting and data-retention claims; certifications and compliance (ISO 27001, GDPR/RGPD, AI Act); customer names, logos and quotes.
  • Don’t announce features or tools before they are released.
  • AgroBot and regulatory content inform; they are not legal advice. Say so.
  • Never: “100% compliant”, “guaranteed”, “zero risk”, “replaces your auditor”.
Privacy / GDPR · RGPD

People and data stay protected.

  • Written consent from every recognisable person in a photo or video, team included. Keep the form in the project folder.
  • Screenshots and recordings use the demo workspace only. No customer names, audit results, emails or personal data.
  • Filming at a customer or partner site needs their written approval first.
  • Blur badges, screens, documents and licence plates in field footage.
  • Customer logos only with written permission, and in their original colours.
Accessibility

Everyone gets the message.

  • Alt text on every image (LinkedIn: “Add alt text”). Describe the message, not the design.
  • Captions on every video, plus the .srt file.
  • Approved colour pairs only (p.09). Minimum type sizes (p.11).
